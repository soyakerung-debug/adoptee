Frequently Asked Questions

We’ve answered the most common questions to help you out.

How does pet adoption work?

+

Browse available pets, choose one you like, and send an adoption request. You’ll then meet the pet before making the final decision.

Is adoption free?

+

Some pets are free, while others may have a small fee to cover basic care like vaccinations and health checks.

Can I meet the pet before adopting?

+

Yes, meeting the pet is encouraged so you can see if it’s a good match for your home and lifestyle.

What if the pet doesn’t suit me?

+

You can explore other pets. Finding the right connection is important, so take your time before deciding.

Are pets vaccinated?

+

Most pets are vaccinated and checked for basic health, but it’s best to confirm details before adoption.
